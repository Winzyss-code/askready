# AskReady

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-specified: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, Vercel, Supabase anonymous auth and Postgres with RLS. Server-side OpenAI transcription, TTS, and an environment-configured LLM wrapper.

## Users
Salaried US professionals aged 25–45 with a compensation conversation coming up.

## Product Purpose
Rehearse a raise conversation, receive grounded coaching, retry a difficult objection, and compare the two answers with the same rubric.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One live scenario. Turn-based voice with an always-available text fallback. No live scoring. Quotes must be real; rewrites must preserve facts. Scores are coaching estimates, not confidence measurements. Free includes one session per day; Pro is an honestly labeled $12.99/month waitlist.

## Product Principles
- Verify each working checkpoint before starting the next.
- Preserve the retry and text fallback.
- Never simulate successful AI, billing, or database operations.
- Keep private keys server-side and user data isolated.

## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied the product requirements. There are no testimonials, measured latency claims, or verified outcome statistics.

## Open Decisions
Final visual identity is deferred until the product flow is built. The checkpoint screen is functional scaffolding, not a committed brand direction.
